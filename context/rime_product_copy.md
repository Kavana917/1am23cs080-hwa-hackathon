## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Freeze.** Pop the sealed Chill Rod in the freezer overnight. No filling, no ice trays.
2. **Load.** Fill the base with cold water, slide on a sleeve, and spread on dog-safe treats or leave it bare for a zero-calorie frost lick.
3. **Lick.** Your dog licks, a small sip of chilled water follows, and the cold sleeve keeps going for 3–4 hours.

## FAQ

**How long does Rime last?**
Rime is designed for a 3–4 hour session at normal indoor room temperature. Time varies with temperature, treat choice, and your dog.

**How do I refreeze it?**
Wash, dry, and freeze the Chill Rod for at least 4 hours (overnight is best) at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

**What can I put on the sleeve?**
Plain unsweetened yogurt, mashed banana, pumpkin puree, or xylitol-free peanut butter. Always check labels for xylitol.

**Will it make a mess?**
The drip-catch collar is designed to hold melting droplets and condensation during a normal session.

**Can my dog chew it?**
No. Rime is a lick toy. If your dog chews silicone, don't use it unattended.

**Is it dishwasher safe?**
Sleeves and collar gasket: top rack, yes. Chill Rod and base: hand-wash only. Never microwave the rod.
