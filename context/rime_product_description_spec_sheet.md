## 1. Product Title and Summary

* **Product Title:** Rime™ — Dual-Action Frozen Lick & Capillary Hydration Column for Dogs
* **Tagline:** *Hydration in its prime.*
* **Main Use:** A tip-resistant enrichment and hydration column that keeps a dog calmly occupied for a **3–4 hour session** while their people are at work, running errands, or out for the afternoon.
* **Core Value (In Simple Words):** Rime pairs a vacuum-insulated cold water base with a reusable, factory-sealed stainless-steel **Rime Chill Rod** and a replaceable honeycomb-textured silicone lick-sleeve. Owners spread dog-safe treats (plain unsweetened yogurt, mashed banana, pumpkin puree, or xylitol-free peanut butter) onto the sleeve, or leave it bare for a zero-calorie frosty lick. A pressure-activated slit-valve gives the dog a small sip of chilled water as they lick. Rime is built for one good session at a time: freeze the rod overnight, load the sleeve, and your dog has a cold, drip-managed activity for **3–4 hours**.
* **Honest scope:** Rime is a session-length enrichment tool, not a replacement for walks, play, fresh water access, or human company. Actual session time depends on room temperature, treat choice, and how fast your dog licks. Designed for typical indoor room temperature (about 68–75°F / 20–24°C).

## 2. Target Buyer and Use Cases

### Target Buyer Persona

* **Primary Buyer:** Working pet parents and remote workers who want a calm, mostly mess-free enrichment activity for a few hours of absence, especially in homes with hardwood floors or rugs.
* **Dog Profiles:**
  * Dogs who get bored or restless when left alone for a few hours.
  * Power lickers who finish frozen Kongs and lick mats in minutes.
  * Dogs in warm homes who enjoy something cold to lick.
  * Flat-faced or heat-sensitive breeds that benefit from a cool lick instead of hard ice cubes (always with supervision and vet guidance for health concerns).

### Problems with Existing Products and How Rime Responds

| Problem with Existing Products | How Rime Responds |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Frozen lick mats and stuffed Kongs** thaw within about 15–25 minutes at room temperature and leave sticky puddles. | A frozen **Rime Chill Rod** sits in the core of the column, keeping the treat sleeve cold and firm for a **3–4 hour session**. |

## 3. Features and Benefits

1. **Sealed Rime Chill Rod (Thermal-Gel Core)**
   * *Feature:* A food-grade stainless-steel cylinder, factory-sealed with purified water and non-toxic, food-grade phase-change gel.
   * *Benefit:* Holds a freezing-level temperature through a 3–4 hour session and never needs to be filled or emptied. Refreeze overnight (minimum 4 hours at 0°F / -18°C or colder).

2. **Pressure-Activated Slit-Valve**
   * *Feature:* A food-grade silicone valve connects the chilled water reservoir to the lick sleeve.
   * *Benefit:* Small sips of water appear only while the dog licks and stop when the dog walks away, so there is no continuous flow.

3. **Dual-Mode Enrichment**
   * *Feature:* Deep grooves hold treat layers, or the sleeve can be left bare for a zero-calorie frost lick.
   * *Benefit:* Use a treat-loaded session for harder days and a plain session for everyday use. The treat layer stays on the outside of the sleeve, away from the water channel.

4. **Drip-Catch Collar**
   * *Feature:* Vertical channels guide melting droplets and condensation into a 60 ml top collar.
   * *Benefit:* Designed to keep floors dry during a normal session. Dogs can lick up what pools in the collar.

5. **Vacuum-Insulated Weighted Base**
   * *Feature:* Double-wall stainless-steel base with a weighted, low-center-of-gravity footprint and non-slip silicone ring.
   * *Benefit:* Keeps water chilled through the session without exterior sweating, and resists sliding or tipping from normal nudges and pawing.

6. **Replaceable Honeycomb Lick-Sleeve**
   * *Feature:* A frosted, food-grade silicone sleeve with deep honeycomb pockets that slides on and off the column in seconds.
   * *Benefit:* The pockets hold yogurt, banana, or xylitol-free peanut butter in place so the lick takes time, and the sleeve comes off for easy cleaning. Replacement sleeves are sold separately when one wears out.

## 4. Dog Safety Information (show on product page, cart, and confirmation)

**Rime is a lick toy, not a chew toy.**

* **Supervise the first uses.** Watch how your dog uses Rime for the first few sessions before leaving them alone with it.
* **Not for chewers.** If your dog bites, chews, or tears at silicone, do not use Rime unattended. Stop use if the sleeve shows cuts, tears, or loose pieces, and replace it.
* **Never give the Chill Rod directly to your dog.** The rod stays inside the sleeve and column at all times.
* **Match the size to your dog.** Standard is designed for small and medium dogs (up to 40 lbs). XL is designed for large and giant dogs (40+ lbs).
* **Check for damage before each session.** Inspect the sleeve, valve, and collar. Replace anything cracked, torn, or loose.
* **Treat safety.** Use only xylitol-free peanut butter and plain, unsweetened yogurt. Check every ingredient for xylitol. Skip treats your dog is allergic to or has been told to avoid.
* **Mind the calories.** Treat-loaded sessions add calories. Use plain frost sessions for everyday use.
* **Talk to your vet** before use if your dog has dental problems, a medical condition, a food allergy, or a restricted diet.
* **Puppies and seniors:** Stay nearby and supervise every session.
* **Not a substitute for care.** Always make sure your dog has access to fresh water, exercise, and company. Do not use Rime as the only thing keeping a dog occupied for longer than 3–4 hours.

### Care and Cleaning (about 60 seconds)

1. **Disassemble:** Unscrew the collar, slide off the sleeve, and unscrew the Chill Rod.
2. **Wash:**
   * *Sleeves and collar gasket:* Top-rack dishwasher safe or warm soapy rinse.
   * *Chill Rod:* Hand-wash the outside, towel dry, then freeze for at least 4 hours at 0°F / -18°C or colder. Do not microwave it or run it through a heated dry cycle.
   * *Base:* Hand-wash the inside with a bottle brush and warm soapy water. Rinse well.
3. **Before each session:** Inspect all parts (see Dog Safety).

### Compatibility and Accessories

* Replacement Honeycomb sleeves come in a Standard fit and an XL fit.
* Spare Chill Rods let you keep one frozen and ready.
* Standard base fits most car cup holders.
